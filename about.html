Technology Transfer

Commercialization & Deployment Strategy

Transitioning academic research prototypes toward sustainable agricultural services

Indicative Model Disclaimer:

Pricing tiers and business projections represent a proposed commercialization framework formulated for university research assessment, not finalized retail subscriptions.

Target User Persona

Persona: Saman Perera (Typical Smallholder)
Demographics: Male farmer, 46 years old, Polonnaruwa District
Cultivation Scale: 3 acres of irrigated paddy (Samba & Nadu cultivars)
Digital Device: Android 10 smartphone with 4G mobile connectivity
Pain Point: Agricultural extension officers visit infrequently; delay in foliar disease identification leads to costly uncalibrated pesticide purchases.

Other key market segments include agricultural extension officers seeking rapid field validation tools and farmer cooperative societies managing collective input procurement.

Core Value Propositions

  • Immediate On-Device Diagnosis: Reduces foliar diagnostic delay from days to under 500 milliseconds.
  • OOD Misdiagnosis Protection: Prevents catastrophic agrochemical misapplication on non-target weeds or unfamiliar bugs.
  • Grad-CAM Visual Transparency: Fosters farmer and extension officer trust by highlighting the exact diseased lesion regions.
  • Spoken Sinhala Interaction: Eliminates literacy barriers through voice-first symptom input and voice synthesized guidance.

Indicative Commercial Model (Freemium SaaS)

Tier 1

Basic Farmer

Free (0 LKR)

Up to 20 multimodal diagnoses per month, basic disease/pest alerts, voice synthesized advisory in Sinhala.

Tier 2

Farmer Pro

LKR 990 / mo

Unlimited diagnoses, complete historical disease tracking across multiple fields, calibrated chemical dosage calculator.

Tier 3

Cooperative / Enterprise

$150 – 500 / mo

Institutional dashboard for extension offices, district epidemic heatmaps, REST API integration with farm ERP software.

Research Assets

Potential Intellectual Property (IP)

Distinct technological contributions identified for intellectual property protection

Core Potential IP Assets:

  • Trained CNN model checkpoint weights and calibrated temperature thresholds for ODIN and OpenMax.
  • Proprietary Sri Lankan field leaf disease and insect pest photographic dataset.
  • Specialized Sinhala agricultural spoken symptom vocabulary and acoustic model adaptations.
  • Deterministic expert safety validation rules and chemical acreage dosage calculation algorithms.

Licensing & Patent Strategy:

The research team will investigate patent applications with the Sri Lanka National Intellectual Property Office (NIPO) for the OOD-integrated agricultural screening pipeline.

The non-commercial codebase will be released under an academic-use license for reproducibility, while maintaining commercialization rights with the research authors.
