Evaluation FAQ

Frequently Asked Academic Questions

Essential inquiries regarding the research scope and system design

Is this the live farmer application?

No. This website is the academic research documentation platform designed for university project assessment, conveying the complete research journey, methodology, and technical architecture.

Why are some benchmark accuracies listed as "Pending"?

In strict adherence to academic integrity, we do not fabricate experimental accuracy figures or AUROC benchmarks. These metrics will be uploaded after laboratory validation and agronomist verification.

Why is Out-of-Distribution (OOD) screening necessary?

Conventional CNNs assume every photo belongs to one of their trained disease classes. When a farmer photographs weeds, soil, or benign insects, traditional models generate dangerous high-confidence false predictions. OOD screening catches and rejects these inputs safely.

How is Sinhala voice assistance integrated?

Spoken Sinhala is captured at 16kHz, transcribed via Whisper/Vosk, parsed for symptom keywords with TF-IDF, classified into foliar disease probabilities via BERT, and returned with synthesized Sinhala voice audio for low-literacy farmers.
