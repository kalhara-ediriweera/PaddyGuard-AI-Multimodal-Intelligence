PaddyGuard AI
Multimodal Intelligence for Rice Disease & Pest Management

An integrated research platform combining deep learning foliar disease classification, field pest detection, Sinhala voice-based symptom understanding, Out-of-Distribution (OOD) reliability screening, and knowledge-driven agricultural advisory.

Research Abstract

"Rice farmers face severe yield losses from foliar diseases and insect infestations while lacking accessible expert consultation. Standard agricultural AI classifiers operate under an unrealistic closed-world assumption—producing confident false predictions on unseen weeds, non-rice pests, or soil backgrounds. PaddyGuard AI addresses these limitations by uniting multimodal interaction (Sinhala voice + imagery) with open-set OOD reliability barriers and verified RAG agricultural decision support."

Academic Architecture

Four Core Research Components

Equally weighted research modules addressing foliar diseases, field pests, low-literacy voice interaction, and verified chemical advisory

01 Speech & NLP (25% Weight)

Voice-Based Rice Disease Diagnosis

Lead: Keshan B K (IT22303820)

Allows smallholder farmers to verbally describe observed crop symptoms in Sinhala. Converts spoken speech into text (Whisper/Vosk), extracts clinical symptom keywords using TF-IDF, and classifies the foliar disease via BERT with synthesized Sinhala voice feedback.

Whisper / Vosk TF-IDF BERT Sinhala TTS PyTorch
Target Classes: Bacterial Blight, Leaf Blast, Brown Spot, Healthy
View Voice Technical Scope →
02 Foliar Vision & OOD (25% Weight)

Rice Leaf Disease Classification + OOD

Lead: Hewanayake H.M.L.M (IT22168740)

Identifies foliar rice diseases from images using transfer learning CNNs (ResNet50, EfficientNetB3, DenseNet121). Implements Out-of-Distribution (OOD) screening via ODIN and OpenMax to reject non-rice leaves and soil, verified by Grad-CAM visual heatmaps.

ResNet50 EfficientNetB3 DenseNet121 ODIN / OpenMax Grad-CAM
Target Classes: Bacterial Blight, Leaf Blast, Brown Spot, Healthy Leaf
View Disease Technical Scope →
04 Pest Vision & OOD (25% Weight)

Rice Pest Detection + OOD

Lead: Kalhara E P K (IT22065858)

Detects and localizes common destructive rice insect pests from field imagery using deep learning (YOLOv8 & CNNs). Deploys OOD screening to identify and reject unfamiliar non-rice insects, preventing unnecessary agrochemical spraying.

YOLOv8 ResNet50 EfficientNet OOD Filtering OpenCV
Target Species: Brown Planthopper, Stem Borer, Leaf Folder, Gall Midge
View Pest Technical Scope →
03 RAG & Expert Engine (25% Weight)

Treatment Advisory & RAG Chatbot

Lead: Weerasinghe KGJP (IT22273680)

A dual-layered decision support system combining Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) over verified IRRI & FAO literature with deterministic expert rules and a land acreage dosage calculator to prevent pesticide hallucinations.

RAG Pipeline FAISS Vector DB LLM Ingestion Expert Rules Dosage Engine
Scope: Chemical & biological recommendations + calibrated sprayer measurements
